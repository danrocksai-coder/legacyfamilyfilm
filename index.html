Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit the form?

You’ll receive an automatic confirmation email letting you know your request was received. Kurt will then personally review your project details and send you the next steps, including your upload link, upload deadline, and estimated timeline.

How do I send my photos and videos?

After Kurt reviews your request, you’ll receive a private upload link where you can submit photos, videos, voice recordings, and written memories for your film.

Can other family members contribute memories?

Yes. The private upload link can be shared with family members so they can upload photos, videos, and written memories that may be meaningful to include in the film.

Is there a deadline for uploading memories?

Yes. Kurt will provide an upload deadline when sending your project instructions. Once that deadline arrives, the project is locked and editing begins.

How long does editing take?

Editing timelines depend on the film package. Typical turnaround times are:

Highlight Film — 3–5 days after the upload deadline
Story Film — 7–10 days after the upload deadline
Legacy Film — 10–14 days after the upload deadline

Will I get to review the film before it’s final?

Yes. You will receive a preview version of your film first. Minor revisions can be made before the final version is delivered.

Meet Kurt

Hi, I’m Kurt.

I studied film editing in college because I’ve always loved storytelling through video. Long before I had a family of my own, I was drawn to the power of editing and the way a well-crafted film can make people feel something real.

Over the years, life gave me even more reason to care about meaningful moments. I’m a husband, a father of three, and someone who understands how quickly the years move. The clips and photos families take every day may seem ordinary now, but later they become priceless.

Legacy Family Film was created to help families preserve those moments. I take years of photos and home videos and turn them into one meaningful film your family can watch and treasure forever.
