How It Works

Simple for you. Professionally handled from start to finish.

  1. Choose your film type — Highlight, Story, or Legacy Film.
  2. Upload your photos and videos — phone clips, old footage, voice notes, and more.
  3. Tell us the story — the occasion, the feeling, and what matters most.
  4. We edit everything into a cinematic film — shaped with care and professional storytelling.
  5. Receive your finished movie — ready to watch, share, gift, or preserve.

Family members can also contribute clips if you want to create a collaborative tribute or group memory film.

About Legacy Family Film

Legacy Family Film exists to help other families turn everyday clips, photos, and stories into something lasting, beautiful, and worth watching again and again.
